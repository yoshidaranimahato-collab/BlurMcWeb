SERVER RULES & GUIDELINES

Please strictly follow these rules to maintain a safe and fair environment on BlurMC.

#1 No Hacking or Unfair Clients
Any use of X-Ray, Auto-Clicker, Killaura, Fly, or modified clients is strictly forbidden and results in a permanent ban.
#2 No Combat Logging
Disconnecting during active PvP combat to save your hearts/items will trigger an automatic death penalties.
#3 Allowed: Trapping & Griefing
Trapping and raiding base builds are ALLOWED in Lifesteal mode. Play carefully and defend your base!
#4 Alt Account Abuse
Using alt accounts to farming extra hearts or circumvent bans is prohibited.
#1 No Griefing Claimed Land
Stealing or destroying builds inside claimed regions is strictly illegal. Respect other players' creations.
#2 Respectful Community Chat
No toxicity, hate speech, spamming, or self-promotion of other servers in public chat.
#3 Lag Machines Prohibited
Building excessive redstone clocks or huge mob farms that deliberately cause TPS drops is not allowed.
#1 Fair Play PvP
Macroing, drag-clicking above 20 CPS, or abusing bugs/glitches will get you instantly temp-banned.
#2 No Elo Boosting
Intentionally throwing matches or boosting stats with friends in ranked matches is forbidden.
#3 Respect Opponents
Keep sportsmanship alive. Toxic behavior or abusive language after matches is punishable.
